# Changelog

## Unreleased – Sprints + Weekly executive report
- **Ideas removed; delete a month safely; notes as comments; backlog bar; month notes (2026-10-25):** the **Ideas** page, its nav item, the roadmap's Ideas drawer and the idea board code are gone (the Monthly **Backlog** replaces it). The `ideas` data collection stays, because shared settings and timeline notes are stored in it. **Delete month** now asks you to **type "delete"** and can be undone with **Undo** (or Ctrl+Z). **Notes** are now a thread like comments: each note shows who wrote it and when, newest at the bottom, with Add note (Ctrl+Enter) and Delete; older single notes show as "Earlier note". A **month column** can have its own notes the same way (icon beside the month name). The **Backlog** is now a full-width bar **above the months** instead of a toolbar button.

- **Monthly: Backlog (2026-10-25):** a collapsed-by-default **Backlog** for ideas and requests that are not planned yet, in the same module. Press **Backlog (n)** in the toolbar to open it as a sticky first column (it stays in view while the months scroll; the choice is remembered on this device). Cards there have ids, tags, flags and notes like any card but no percentage. The business plans from it by **dragging a card into a month** or, without dragging, **⋯ → Plan in month → <month>**; **⋯ → Back to backlog** sends a planned card back. Design follows common backlog patterns (a collapsible panel next to the board, progressive disclosure, and a menu alternative to dragging as WCAG 2.5.7 asks). Stored as the doc `reports/mobacklog` (`mo:'backlog'`); it is not a month, so it never appears in month totals, copy text or the weekly report.

- **Monthly: drag auto-scroll (2026-10-25):** while a card is being dragged, the page scrolls up or down by itself when the pointer is near the top or bottom edge (and the board scrolls sideways near its left and right edges), so a card can be dragged from the top of a long column to the bottom or to a far month in one move.

- **Monthly: Undo, move fix, two on-premise tags (2026-10-25):** **Undo** button (and Ctrl+Z) on the Monthly board steps back through your changes (add, edit, move, delete, tag, flags, notes; moving a card to another month is one step). **Bug fixed:** moving a card within the same column could delete it, because the card was removed and re-added in two separate saves; it is now one save (and across months the card is added to the new month first, then removed). The tag button on every card now shows a **▾ arrow**; click it to pick another tag from the dropdown at any time. New tags **SCHS** (#2e818d, SC) and **Warif** (#5dd9d2, WR) for the on-premise products.

- **Monthly: Add to roadmap, card ids, note bubble (2026-10-25):** the **Sync from Roadmap** button and menu item are gone. Instead a card's ⋯ menu has **Add to roadmap…**: pick the assignee, squad, start and end date and the card becomes a bar on the open roadmap, linked to the card (it then shows an "On roadmap" chip and "Show on roadmap"). Every card gets an id for tracking, **tag abbreviation # number** (DC#12, TF#3, PL#7, AI, FN, NI); click it to copy; the number stays when the tag changes, older cards were numbered once, and the search also finds ids. A card with a note now shows the **green icon with a red bubble** to grab attention.

- **Monthly tags split (2026-10-25):** the board has its own six tags, chosen from a dropdown on every card (click the coloured tag at the top of the card) or from the chips when adding a card: **Daycare** (pink), **Tifli** (purple), **Plan** (green), **AI** (blue), **Finance** (grey), **New initiative** (gold). Plan and Daycare are no longer one combined tag. Sync from Roadmap puts a feature in the tag of its squad (daycare, tifli, plan, ai); features of other squads are counted as "have no tag". Cards made before the split are converted automatically when an editor opens the board (the old Plan / Daycare card becomes Plan, or follows the squad of its roadmap bar). The weekly report keeps its own products.

- **48-hour sessions; Business priority; small-enhancement star (2026-10-25):** nobody is signed out after 30 minutes any more: a user stays signed in until **48 hours without activity** (counted from the last activity of that browser, even if the tab was closed, or from the sign-in if newer), with the same 60-second "Still there?" warning before sign-out. On the Monthly board the gold flag is now called **Business priority** (a gold ribbon with that text across the card, filter and copy text use the same name). The **Mark as ad hoc request** item is gone from the card menu (cards already marked keep their tag, with a way back to planned). Next to the flag every card has a **star** button: one click marks a **Small enhancement** (purple star, tag on the card, filter "Small enhancements"); **Quick win** stays in the card menu.

- **Monthly cards: flags and notes (2026-10-25):** a **gold flag** on a card marks it **high priority** (one click on/off, gold ring around the card, shown to executives as "High priority"). The card menu has **Quick win** and **Enhancement** (one at a time, click again to remove) so the business can see which items are small. A **note icon** on every card opens a box; once a note exists the icon turns green and hovering (or focusing) it shows the note as a tooltip, for executives too. A new filter next to the product filter shows only High priority, Quick wins or Enhancements. Copy text adds [HIGH PRIORITY], [QUICK WIN] / [ENHANCEMENT] and the note.

- **Monthly board stays live while you type (2026-10-25):** a card added or changed by someone else now appears even while you have the "Add a card" box open (what you were typing is kept). The page only holds off refreshing while you are editing a card's title, a dropdown or a search box.

- **Monthly deliverables, Trello-style (2026-10-25):** each column ends with **+ Add a card**: it opens a composer with the products as colour chips (pick one, the box takes its colour); **Enter** adds the card and keeps the box open for the next one, **Esc** closes it, and a pasted list becomes one card per line. The product label on a card is a button: click it to pick another product and the whole card is repainted in that colour. Cards can be **dragged to any position**, inside a month (re-order, with a drop line) or into another month.

- **Monthly deliverables board; Capacity and Baselines removed (2026-10-25):** the **Capacity** and **Baselines** pages are gone, with the "Plan vs now" ghost bars (the `baselines` table and its data stay in the database, unused). **Monthly deliverables** is now a board like Ideas: one column per month starting with this month (October 2026), then November, and **+ Add month** adds the next one; **Show past months** brings back earlier ones. Each card is a deliverable coloured by its product (change the product on the card to change the colour), with a 0-100 percentage in steps of 10, a progress bar per month, a note when it came from the roadmap, an Ad hoc tag, move to another month (⋯ menu or drag), delete, search and a product filter. **Sync from Roadmap** is per month: it brings the roadmap features that END in that month, and never repeats a feature: bars with the same title in the same product (a feature written on several people) become one card, and a feature already on the board in any month is not added again. Executives see every month read-only. Months are always saved as submitted (the old Submit / Reopen buttons and the month sheet are gone).

- **Weekly report clean-up (2026-10-21):** items are **plain text** (no rich formatting; the formatting bar is gone). The Hide button is gone: the ✕ on an item deletes it. New **Out of scope** flag per item (red tag) for work added to the sprint over the planned roadmap; shown to executives, in the PDF and in Copy text. A product section can be **renamed** (type in its title) and **removed** (any product, including AI (Sara)); removed sections stay removed after a sync. **Save** replaces "Submit to executives": it shows the report to the executives and the report stays editable (changes are saved automatically and the executives see them); there is no Reopen. **Delete report** is only in the ⋯ menu of the list and only for people with edit access (run `supabase/sql/07-reports-editor-delete.sql` so the database allows editors to delete a saved report). **Print / PDF** now builds a dedicated document: header with dates and label, overall progress, a card per product, then each product's items with progress bars, out-of-scope tags and notes, page numbers in the footer.
- **Basic formatting that always works (2026-10-21):** the per-product bar now has only **B** (bold), **A−** / **A+** (text size) and **Normal**. It formats the whole item you last clicked, with or without a text selection (the old selection-based buttons did not work reliably in every browser). Saved with the item and shown to executives.
- **Weekly report: progress from its own items, product sections, notes, per-product formatting (2026-10-21):** the progress block at the top (overall and one bar per product, coloured by the same bands) is now calculated from the items written in the report (average of the items; 0% until items are added) instead of the Monthly page; the "Open the month" link and the summary tiles are gone. **+ Product section** adds a section to that report only (own colour, its own bar, can be removed); sync keeps it. The report's **dates and label are editable** (From, To, and a free label such as "Sprint 229 - week 1"). The AI button was removed (it only wrote the summaries). Each line's dropdown is now a percentage (0 to 100 in steps of 10; yellow 1 to 50, orange 51 to 99, green 100; 100 marks the line done), shown as a coloured chip to executives. Summaries stay empty until written. Toolbar spacing on the report and Monthly pages improved.

## Google Drive: one-click export to a real Google Doc
