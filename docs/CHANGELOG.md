# Changelog

## Unreleased – Sprints + Weekly executive report

- **Dashboard simpler, and names follow Resources (2026-10-27):** the people of each track now come from **Resources** (Dashboard settings: each track shows its people as chips and a "Add a person" list of everyone in Resources; no typing). Khalid Nazim is in PMs and Khalid Abdelfattah in Design as two different people (Design shows the name as Resources lists him until he is renamed there). Default names are matched to Resources (for example Hamid is Hamid Shahin); names Resources does not list are called out under What needs fixing with an **Add them to Resources** button (editors). Tests: `monthly_capacity.py` (58) and unit checks.

- **Charts on the Dashboard (2026-10-27):** the Dashboard now has four charts, drawn with plain HTML and SVG (no library): a **heat map of tracks by month** (darker = fuller; each box shows the share and points planned / points the track can take; over the limit is red with a triangle and the word, never colour alone), **columns** of points planned each month against the capacity of all tracks (dark tick), **bars** track by track for one month (pale bar = what the track can take, green = planned, red = past the limit) and a **heat map of people by month** (planned / can take per person; a dash for someone who left or is away all month). One **month filter** above the charts drives the bars and the team. Hover or keyboard focus shows a tooltip with the numbers, and every chart has a **Table** button that swaps it for the same numbers as a table. One green ramp (light to dark) for fullness; red only for over. People over what they can take are now one line under What needs fixing. The old "Room left" table became the track heat map. Tests: `monthly_capacity.py` (54).

- **Points per person, and the module is now called Dashboard (2026-10-27):** the card menu item is **Points and people…** and works in three steps: (1) choose the **tracks** that work on the card, (2) for each chosen track tick **who** works on it, (3) give **each person** their points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13). The track total is the sum of its people and is what the capacity uses, so nothing else changes; the card's points chip lists the names on hover. The former Capacity page is now **Dashboard** (menu, page title, Monthly button, Dashboard settings). In **The team** section a month picker shows what each person has planned against what they can take (after leave, reserve and buffer); someone over is flagged and listed under What needs fixing. Cards that only had track totals open with their tracks chosen and ask for the people. Card field `who` {track: {name: points}} next to `pts`; no SQL. Tests: `monthly_capacity.py` (43) and unit checks.

- **Capacity dashboard (2026-10-27):** the capacity meters moved out of the Monthly columns into their own page, **Capacity** (menu, between Monthly and Resources; same access as Monthly, executives read-only). It has four plain sections: **The months at a glance** (a tile per month: Fits / Tight / Over, which tracks, points planned), **What needs fixing** (each role over capacity with a suggested card to move to a later month where it fits, cards that need a card planned later, roadmap bars outside their month; each with an "Open month in Monthly" button), **Room left in each track** (used / can take and free points per track and month, coloured green, amber, red) and **The team** (each track with its people; on leave today with the end date, coming leave, leaving soon, left, and people who sit in two tracks). **Capacity settings** (editors, on this page) now also take **Leaving or left** (name: last working day): a person leaves the capacity after that day. Monthly keeps the points on cards, the Points menu and the warning before a move, plus a Capacity button. Same data, no SQL: `ideas/capacity` gains `leaving`. Tests: `monthly_capacity.py` (31) and unit checks.

- **Monthly capacity in points, per role (2026-10-27):** every card can carry **points by role** (card menu > Points by role…: AI, Full Stack FE, Full Stack Mobile, BE, PMs, Design, Quality; scale 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) and **needs** another card (planned in the same month or earlier). Under each month heading, **capacity meters** show points planned against what the role can take: people in the role (November 2026 list) x points per person (default 8) less their leave from Vacations, less a 20% reserve (bugs, support) and a 10% buffer; green, amber above 90%, red over. **Moving a card** (menu or drag) that would put a role over capacity, or break the order of cards that need each other, asks first (Move anyway / Cancel). **Check plan** lists all conflicts: roles over capacity, cards needing a card planned later, and linked roadmap bars outside the card's month. **Capacity settings** (editors) set points per person, reserve, buffer and who is in each role. Executives see meters, points and Check plan read-only. Points are estimated by the team, never by the business; no per-person assignment. Settings doc `ideas/capacity`; card fields `pts`, `needs`. Tests: `monthly_capacity.py` (15) and capacity unit checks.

- **A save can no longer wipe cards added by someone else (2026-10-27):** each Monthly save used to write the whole month from the screen's own copy, so a screen that had not refreshed (or was offline during the data-API outage) could overwrite cards another person added. Now, before every save, the stored month is read and **cards the screen has never seen are kept** (a message says how many), and saves of the same month go one after another. Edits to the same card are still last-writer-wins. Test `monthly_merge_save.py` (5). No database change.

- **Recovery from a data-API outage (2026-10-27):** the login stall was Supabase's data API (PostgREST) returning 503 PGRST002 with the database healthy; fixed by restarting the project. The "Taking longer than expected" screen now **retries by itself every 15 s** and opens the tool as soon as the server answers, so nobody has to refresh. New runbook and prevention list in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` (uptime monitor on the API, Supabase alerts, plan upgrade, pre-demo check). Test `login_hang.py` now 9 checks.

- **Login screen no longer loads forever (2026-10-27):** the sign-in check and the profile lookup had no time limit, so when the server did not answer (network, paused or slow Supabase project, a stale saved sign-in) the page stayed on "Loading…". They now give up after 10-12 seconds and show **Taking longer than expected** with **Try again** and **Sign out and start fresh** (clears the saved sign-in on this browser). Test `login_hang.py` (6).

- **Link a sprint task to Jira by searching (2026-10-26):** on a sprint plan, the line options (⋮⋮) of a scope or sub-section now have **Link to Jira…** (or **Change Jira link**). The task then shows the Jira key as a link that opens the issue, takes the Jira status, and can be unlinked. Read-only: nothing is changed in Jira. New Edge Function **`jira-search`** (editors only, same three secrets as `jira-sync`, **must be deployed**); the Jira site address is remembered from Pull from Jira / search so keys become links. Tests: `jira_link.py` (14).

- **Users & permissions; Undo only takes back your own changes; Monthly subtitle removed (2026-10-25):** the admin page is now **Users & permissions**. Pick a person on the side and see a card per module (Roadmap, Roadmaps, Sprints, Reports, Monthly deliverables, Resources, Vacations, Log) with **View / Edit / Delete** switches (Delete includes Edit, Edit includes View; Log is view-only). The app follows the levels: pages without View are hidden (and a typed address falls back), editing needs Edit on that page's module, Delete actions (delete card, month, roadmap, sprint, vacation, team member, bar) need Delete. The Monthly page no longer has the long subtitle.
